# OctoAcme Project Management Docs

## OctoAcme Project Management Processes

OctoAcme follows a lightweight project lifecycle: initiation validates the business need, measurable outcomes, stakeholders, timeline, risks, and resources; planning turns approved work into a prioritized backlog with acceptance criteria, estimates, dependencies, a Definition of Done, and milestones. Teams execute in small increments, track work across project-board stages, then release with staging checks, production verification, and a rollback or mitigation plan. Retrospectives capture lessons and assign follow-up improvements.

The Project Manager coordinates delivery, schedules, risks, dependencies, and communications. The Product Manager defines outcomes, prioritizes work, and measures success. Developers implement and test features, collaborate on design and reviews, and identify technical risks. QA validates quality and acceptance criteria, while stakeholders provide input and approvals.

Teams use standups to surface progress and blockers, weekly delivery or PM/Product Manager syncs to review status and risks, and regular stakeholder updates to maintain alignment. A shared source of truth supports transparent status reporting; blockers escalate from the team through the Project Manager and Product Lead to the sponsor as needed. Incident communications summarize triage, actions, and expected timelines.

Quality is integrated throughout delivery: new logic receives unit tests, integration tests are added where applicable, and critical flows get end-to-end smoke tests before release. Pull requests should be small where possible, link to issues and acceptance criteria, and pass automated tests, linting, and security scans before review; at least one approval is expected unless team policy differs. Manual QA, post-deployment checks, and blameless retrospectives support reliable releases and continuous improvement.
