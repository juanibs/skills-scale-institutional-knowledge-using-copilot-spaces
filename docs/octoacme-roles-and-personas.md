# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## Executive Sponsors

### Role Summary
Executive Sponsors champion an initiative, ensure it remains aligned with organizational priorities, and help secure the support and resources needed to deliver it.

### Responsibilities
- Confirm the initiative's strategic value and desired business outcomes
- Secure or escalate decisions about funding, staffing, and organizational priority
- Resolve escalations that are beyond the delivery team's authority
- Review progress and outcomes at agreed milestones

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Align with the Product Manager on desired outcomes and priority
- Partner with the Project Manager on sponsorship, resource needs, and escalations
- Receive progress, risks, and decision requests from the Project Manager
- Consult stakeholders when business impact or organizational alignment is at issue

---

## Business Analysts

### Role Summary
Business Analysts clarify business needs and translate them into requirements that delivery teams and stakeholders can understand and validate.

### Responsibilities
- Elicit and document business requirements, workflows, and constraints
- Help define clear, testable acceptance criteria
- Trace requirements to intended outcomes and identify gaps or conflicts
- Support validation of delivered functionality against agreed requirements

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Work with the Product Manager to refine scope, backlog items, and acceptance criteria
- Coordinate with stakeholders to understand needs and confirm requirements
- Clarify requirements for Developers and QA/Testing throughout implementation and validation
- Keep the Project Manager informed about requirement changes that affect scope, dependencies, or schedule

---

## Technical Leads / Architects

### Role Summary
Technical Leads and Architects guide technical design and help ensure that implementation choices meet product needs and engineering standards.

### Responsibilities
- Define or review technical designs, architecture, and integration approaches
- Identify technical dependencies, constraints, and risks
- Advise on estimates, sequencing, and maintainability
- Support consistent engineering practices and resolve technical questions

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Collaborate with Developers on design, implementation, and technical trade-offs
- Advise the Product Manager and Project Manager about feasibility, dependencies, and delivery impact
- Work with QA/Testing to plan testability and address technical quality risks
- Coordinate with Stakeholders when technical constraints affect agreed needs or outcomes

---

## Release Managers

### Role Summary
Release Managers coordinate release readiness and the activities needed to deploy changes safely and communicate them clearly.

### Responsibilities
- Coordinate release scope, readiness checks, and deployment timing
- Confirm release notes, rollout steps, and rollback or mitigation plans are prepared
- Track release blockers and coordinate their resolution
- Ensure deployment and post-release verification activities have clear owners

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Coordinate with the Project Manager on release milestones, dependencies, and stakeholder communications
- Confirm acceptance criteria and release scope with the Product Manager
- Work with Developers and QA/Testing to verify readiness and smoke-test plans
- Coordinate with Operations / Support on deployment, monitoring, and handoff

---

## Operations / Support Representatives

### Role Summary
Operations and Support Representatives help ensure that delivered changes can be operated, monitored, and supported effectively.

### Responsibilities
- Identify operational requirements, support impacts, and readiness gaps
- Advise on monitoring, alerting, runbooks, and incident-response needs
- Prepare support teams for changes and document relevant handoffs
- Share operational feedback and post-release issues with the delivery team

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Partner with Developers and Technical Leads / Architects to plan operability and observability
- Coordinate with Release Managers on deployment readiness and post-release verification
- Inform the Project Manager about operational risks and support dependencies
- Provide the Product Manager and Stakeholders with feedback about customer-impacting issues

---

## Security / Privacy Partners

### Role Summary
Security and Privacy Partners help teams identify and address security, privacy, and data-protection requirements and risks.

### Responsibilities
- Advise on applicable security, privacy, and data-handling requirements
- Identify risks and recommend appropriate controls or mitigations
- Review relevant designs, changes, and readiness evidence
- Escalate unresolved or significant risks through established channels

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Work with the Product Manager and Stakeholders to clarify security and privacy expectations
- Advise Developers and Technical Leads / Architects on secure design and implementation
- Coordinate with QA/Testing on relevant verification and test coverage
- Keep the Project Manager informed about risks, remediation owners, and schedule or release impacts

---

## How these personas are used in the exercise
- Use these persona definitions to frame scenarios and sample interactions in the Skills Exercise.
- Each persona can be used as a persona prompt for Copilot Spaces to shape role-specific guidance.
- Assign or combine these roles according to project size, while keeping ownership of each responsibility explicit.
